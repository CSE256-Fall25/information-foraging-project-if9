Language Development & Spanish

Language development is enhanced through:

  • Verbally communicating and listening to others
  • Dramatic play
  • Listening to and telling stories
  • Listening to music and singing songs
  • Beginning to identify meaningful words — own name, friends' names, calendar words, job chart, etc.
  • Dictating, drawing, and writing stories, and creating books of the child's own making


Spanish with Miss Colleen

Colleen Corbett

AB English, Minor in Spanish, MAEd Counseling

¡Hola! (Hello!) de Señora Colleen (from Miss Colleen)

I am excited to begin my seventh year teaching the Spanish enrichment program at WUNS. My philosophy in teaching Spanish is to keep it fun, simple, and multi-sensory. Whether through song, props, games, or movement, I like to introduce vocabulary and simple phrases in memorable ways.

Once a week I visit each classroom for 15–20 minutes for songs, games, and stories in Spanish. On this website, I list vocabulary, song lyrics, and lesson overviews. Repetition is key to learning, so I revisit lessons throughout the year for review and recall.
