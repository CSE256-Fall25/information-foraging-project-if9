The Bear Cub Class

The Bear Cub class is the morning session for 3- and 4-year-old children. Their growing communication and motor skills allow them to become more self-sufficient. Teacher guidance and classroom routines help build confidence as children enjoy individual, small-group, and large-group activities.

Classroom Goals

Our teachers encourage students to:

  • Cultivate a positive self-image, both as individuals and as members of the classroom community.
  • Form positive, constructive relationships with others while building friendships and working together.
  • Develop independence in a supportive group setting.
  • Respect and care for their learning environment.
  • Communicate openly and honestly with peers, teachers, and staff.
  • Discover the joy of learning and see school as fun.
